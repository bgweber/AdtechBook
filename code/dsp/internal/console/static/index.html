Train a click model

Builds click examples from the ledger, trains logistic regression with FTRL on every day but the last, evaluates on the last day, and publishes the model with its golden examples and validation report (Chapters 9 and 11). Takes a few seconds at tiny scale. You can train while the simulator runs: a day still in progress is left out.

Install labels

    

      
      Joins the MMP's postbacks to our impressions: how long install labels take
        to arrive, how complete each day's labels are so far, and, with the simulator's truth, how many of the
        installs credited to us our ads actually caused (Chapter 13).
    

  

  

    
Share of installs received, by hours after the impression

    

    
Click-through installs arrive within hours; view-through installs take longer, because the
      device installs later, without a click. A model trained on yesterday's impressions sees only the labels that
      have arrived.

  

  

    
Label maturity by day of impression

    

    
The share of each day's installs, so far, that had arrived by the end of that day and each day
      after. A dash is a day that hasn't happened yet.

  

  

    
Attributed and caused installs, by placement

    

    
Attributed: installs the MMP credited to us. Caused: installs our ads caused, from the
      simulator's truth; some of them the MMP credited to another network. The caused shares are the part of our
      credited installs that the ads caused, rather than installs that would have happened anyway.

Campaign selection

    

      Serving: –
      
      The internal auction prices every eligible campaign and bids for the one with the most expected
        surplus: the chance of winning times its calibrated value minus the bid (Chapter 21). 10% of requests pick a
        campaign at random instead, so the logged selection probabilities stay useful offline. Trace a bid with
        adtrain trace to see every campaign's score.

Auction candidate cap

    

      Now: –
      
      With a cap of k, the internal auction prices only the k campaigns with the best
        recent scores, so models and shading run k times instead of once per eligible campaign. During a load test, 5%
        of capped auctions are priced again in full, to measure what the cap gives up. 0 prices every campaign.

      fallback pricing many bids, the click predictions collapsing or jumping, latency, and a reconciliation gap.

Prediction monitor

    
Each model's mean prediction over the last simulated hour (line), against the
      range of the same hour on up to four days before (band) and their median (dashed). Vertical lines are deploys,
      rollbacks, and drills from the audit log. The shift alert fires when the mean is more than 4 robust standard
      deviations, and 5%, from the median, for three quarter hours in a row (Chapter 24).

Incident drills

      
A bad deploy
The serving conversion bundle's click predictions
        multiplied, as a bad build would. A rollback to any other bundle ends it.

        
        

      
A serving bug
The bidder's code for connection_type changes:
        finer cellular types than training expects. The daily parity check finds it.

        

      
Bad install labels
From the simulator: the MMP credits false
        installs for half a day. Run ADSIM_SCENARIO=bad-labels docker compose run --rm sim.

      

      
Daily parity check 
        
Runs when the simulated day turns, on the day that just ended.
